IBAN Encryption Keypair Generator

Generates a Curve25519 keypair for the club's IBAN encryption (ADR-0036). Runs entirely in this page — no network, nothing stored, nothing sent. Close the tab and the keys are gone unless you copied them.

Compare this against the fingerprint the admin panel shows after registering.

The private key is the only way to ever read the club's IBANs again. Store it in the club's secure archive (password manager entry or sealed envelope in the safe), document who may access it, and test the recovery copy periodically. Losing the last private key makes every IBAN sealed under it unrecoverable. It is needed only for SEPA exports and key rotation — it is never stored on the server.
